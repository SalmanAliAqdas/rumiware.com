Questions

Frequently asked questions

Should I build for Android, iOS or both?

Most businesses in Pakistan start with Android because more people use it. Flutter lets you cover both with one project, and we will advise you based on your customers.

How long does an app take?

A simple app can take four to eight weeks. Bigger apps take longer, and you get a clear timeline before we start.

Do you publish the app to the stores?

Yes. We prepare the listing and publish to Google Play and the App Store. Store fees are paid directly by you.

Can the app work with my website?

Yes. We can connect the app to your website, database or payment system so everything stays in sync.
