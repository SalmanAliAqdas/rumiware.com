Frequently asked questions

How long does a website take?

A simple business website usually takes two to three weeks. Online stores and web apps take longer, and we give a clear timeline before we start.

Can I update the content myself?

Yes. We can build it so you edit text and images easily, or we can handle updates for you under a support plan.

Will my site work on mobile phones?

Always. We design for phones first, then tablets and computers, and test on all of them.

Do you help with hosting and a domain?

Yes. We help you choose, set up and connect hosting and your domain name, and explain each step simply.
