Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy describes the website features currently used by Rumiware, a software house in {{LOCATION}}.

Information you send

The contact and quote forms send the information you enter, such as your name, email, optional phone number and project details, to Formspree for processing. We use these enquiries to respond to you and discuss your project. You can also contact us directly by email or WhatsApp. Newsletter subscriptions and job applications are currently unavailable.

Local storage and analytics

We use your browser's local storage to remember your theme (rw-theme) and versioned analytics preference (rw-analytics-v1). These settings stay in that browser until you clear them. Essential website features work independently of any analytics choice.

Google Analytics 4 loads only after you select Accept analytics. Before a choice or after Decline analytics, we load no Google Analytics scripts and send no analytics requests. Older saved acceptance does not grant permission for this implementation. Your choice applies to this browser and website origin; clearing site data resets it.

After acceptance, Google receives basic page measurement, including the public page address and title, a referrer without query parameters or fragments, browser/device information and technical network information such as your IP address. Google Analytics uses first-party _ga cookies to distinguish visits; we limit their lifetime to 400 days. Google processes this information under its Privacy Policy, potentially outside your country. Advertising storage, advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled.

We send one page_view per accepted page load. The analytics tag runs in a separate, hidden document containing no forms or chat content. We do not send names, emails, phone numbers, enquiry text or chat messages to Analytics. Page addresses use known public routes; queries, fragments and campaign parameters from the address bar are excluded. Development and preview sites do not activate tracking.

Use to change your choice. Decline analytics withdraws consent, stops future collection and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies on known domain and path scopes. This does not retract data already sent to Google. We cannot remove cookies belonging to another domain or cookies that browser scripts cannot access. Forms, chat and the rest of the website work regardless of your analytics choice. You can also clear site data in your browser settings.

External services

  • Formspree: receives contact and quote submissions and technical request information. A successful form response means the provider accepted the request; it does not confirm email delivery.
  • Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque and DM Sans are served from this website. Page loads do not contact Google Fonts.
  • Google Maps: the contact page embeds a Google map. Loading that embed contacts Google and may use Google's cookies. It operates separately from your optional analytics choice.
  • Hosting: the hosting provider receives technical information needed to serve pages. The repository also provides a GitHub Pages preview.
  • External links: opening WhatsApp, GitHub or an email service takes you to that service, which handles information under its own policies.
