Our story

Built on curiosity and trust

Rumiware began with a simple idea: good software should be honest, beautiful and affordable for businesses of every size. The name comes from Rumi, the poet whose words about love, patience and craft still inspire us.

Today we are a focused team of four: a CEO, a data scientist, and two full-stack developers who also build mobile apps and AI automation. We work closely with every client, so you always talk to the people actually building your product.
