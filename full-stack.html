Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does full-stack mean?

It means we build everything you see (the screens) and everything behind the scenes (the server, database and hosting) so the product works as one system.

Can you improve a product that already exists?

Yes. We review what you have, tell you honestly what is worth keeping and then fix, extend or rebuild it.

Who owns the code?

You do. When the project is paid for, the code and accounts belong to you, with clear documentation.

Will it handle more users later?

We plan for growth from the start, so your product can handle more users without being rebuilt.

Ready to build your product?

Share your idea or your current system. We will suggest a clear plan and a fair quote.
