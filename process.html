How we work

A clear process from idea to launch

Six simple steps, so you always know what is happening, what comes next and what it costs.

01

Discovery

We learn about your business, goals and customers, then agree on what to build, the budget and the timeline.

Typically 2-3 days

  • A short call or WhatsApp chat
  • Clear scope and fixed quote
  • Simple project plan
02

Design

We create layouts and clickable prototypes so you can see and approve the look before any code is written.

Typically 3-7 days

  • Wireframes and visual design
  • Your feedback and changes
  • Approved design
03

Development

We build your product in steps and share progress often, so there are no surprises at the end.

Depends on the project

  • Regular progress updates
  • Working demo links
  • Clean, documented code
04

Testing

We check every page and feature on phones, tablets and computers, and fix problems before you see them.

Typically 2-5 days

  • Device and browser checks
  • Speed and security checks
  • Your final review
05

Launch

We take your product live smoothly, connect your domain and hosting, and make sure everything works.

Typically 1-2 days

  • Go-live checklist
  • Domain and hosting setup
  • Handover and training
06

Support

We stay with you after launch to fix issues, keep things secure and add improvements as you grow.

Ongoing

  • Bug fixes and updates
  • Security and backups
  • Optional monthly support plan
💬

Clear communication

Updates by WhatsApp, email or call, whichever you prefer.

💰

No hidden costs

You approve the quote and any change before work begins.

🔑

You own everything

Code, designs and accounts belong to you once the project is paid for.
