Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains what information Rumiware ("we", "us") collects on rumiware.com, how we use it and the choices you have. We are a software house based in Lahore, Pakistan.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: when you fill in our contact, quote, careers or newsletter forms, we receive what you type, such as your name, email, phone number, company, project details and links you share.
  • Messages you send us: by email or WhatsApp, if you contact us there.
  • Simple browser storage: we save your light or dark theme choice and your cookie choice on your own device.

How we use it

  • To reply to your messages and prepare quotes
  • To review job applications
  • To send newsletter emails, only if you subscribed
  • To keep our website secure and working properly

We do not sell your personal information.

Services we use

To run this website we rely on trusted third parties, including GitHub Pages (hosting), Formspree or a similar service (to deliver form messages to us) and Google Fonts (to display our fonts). These services may receive technical information such as your IP address. Please see their own privacy policies for details.

Cookies and storage

We use only basic storage to remember your preferences. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy and ask for your choice first. You can clear stored data any time in your browser settings.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiries and project information for as long as needed to serve you and meet our legal and business needs, then delete it or make it anonymous.

Your choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to unsubscribe from emails, by writing to us at the address below.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of sending data over the internet is completely secure.

Children

Our website is for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email info@rumiware.com or visit our contact page.
