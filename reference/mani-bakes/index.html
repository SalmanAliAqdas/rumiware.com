Meet our chef

Hi, I am Hajra Mani, the baker behind Mani Bakes. What started as baking for family has grown into a home kitchen that serves our neighbours and friends. Every cake is mixed, baked and decorated by hand.

We use fresh butter, real cream and quality chocolate. If you can imagine a design, we will try to bake it.

How to order

  1. Choose items from the menu and fill in the order form.
  2. Confirm on WhatsApp. We reply with the final price and delivery charges.
  3. Pay by Easypaisa, JazzCash or cash.
  4. Enjoy your fresh order at your door or pick it up.

Please order custom cakes at least 2 days ahead. Same-day orders depend on availability.
