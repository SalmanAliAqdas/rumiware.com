Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy describes the website features currently used by Rumiware, a software house in Lahore, Pakistan.

Information you send

The contact and quote forms send the information you enter, such as your name, email, optional phone number and project details, to Formspree for processing. We use these enquiries to respond to you and discuss your project. You can also contact us directly by email or WhatsApp. Newsletter subscriptions and job applications are currently unavailable.

Local storage and analytics

We use your browser's local storage to remember your theme (rw-theme) and privacy preference (rw-cookie). These settings stay in that browser until you clear them. Essential website features work independently of any analytics choice.

Optional analytics, including Google Analytics, are disabled: this version does not load Google's analytics script or send analytics events, even if an older visit saved an Accept choice. On each page we attempt to remove accessible Google Analytics cookies left by earlier versions. We cannot remove cookies belonging to another domain or cookies that browser scripts cannot access.

Use to revisit your choice or withdraw an older acceptance. You can also clear site data using your browser settings.

External services

  • Formspree: receives contact and quote submissions and technical request information. A successful form response means the provider accepted the request; it does not confirm email delivery.
  • Google Fonts: fonts are requested from Google's servers when a page loads. These requests share technical information such as your IP address with Google.
  • Google Maps: the contact page embeds a Google map. Loading that embed contacts Google and may use Google's cookies. It operates separately from the disabled analytics feature.
  • Hosting: the hosting provider receives technical information needed to serve pages. The repository also provides a GitHub Pages preview.
  • External links: opening WhatsApp, GitHub or an email service takes you to that service, which handles information under its own policies.

Your information and choices

For questions about information you have sent, or to request access, correction or deletion, email info@rumiware.com. Avoid sending sensitive personal information through these forms.

Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email info@rumiware.com or visit our contact page.
